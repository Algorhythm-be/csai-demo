How we hire

No riddles.
No ten rounds.

  1. Get to know each other.

    A 30-minute online introduction about the job and your background.

  2. A real problem.

    We talk through a case like the ones we work on. No trick questions, no whiteboard puzzles.

  3. Meet the team.

    You talk to the people you'd work with, and you decide if it fits.

  4. The offer.

    When we're both happy. We'll make you an offer.
