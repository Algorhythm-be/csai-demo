Practical questions

Before we start.

Can you improve an existing AI prototype?

Yes. We can assess an existing prototype, identify gaps in evaluation or integration, and agree the work needed to take it further. The next step depends on the current system and the workflow it needs to support.

Can an AI solution run on premises?

An on-premises deployment can be considered where security, infrastructure and model requirements allow it. Our self-service data case describes a local deployment at a public institution. We assess your requirements before recommending a deployment approach.

How do you evaluate accuracy and uncertain answers?

We agree evaluation criteria around the real task and test representative examples, including difficult cases. Depending on the workflow, the solution can flag uncertainty, request clarification or route output for human review. These behaviours need to be designed and tested explicitly.

Who maintains the solution after launch?

We agree operational ownership, monitoring, documentation and any support scope as part of the engagement. Those responsibilities may sit with your team, with us under an agreed arrangement, or be shared. They should be clear before launch.
