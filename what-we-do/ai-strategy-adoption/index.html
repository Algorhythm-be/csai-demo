The work

Connect the business case to the daily work.

We work with leadership, project managers, business teams and developers to turn different expectations into a shared plan.

01

Use-case prioritisation

Assess opportunities against the task or decision they should improve. We consider the potential benefit, available data, integration effort and consequences of an incorrect result. That gives stakeholders a basis for choosing what to investigate first.

02

Readiness and ownership

Identify the data, access, expertise and decisions a project depends on. Define the business owner, the people who will use the output and the team responsible for running it. Unresolved dependencies become explicit next steps.

03

Adoption around real tasks

Build skills through the work people actually do. We help teams understand suitable uses, limitations and review responsibilities, then agree how to assess whether a new tool or workflow is being used effectively.

Getting started

A clear starting point.

We use the first conversation to understand the question, identify who needs to be involved and agree a suitable next step. Scope, timing and commercial terms follow from that discussion.

Practical questions

Before we start.

Do we need a clear AI project before talking to you?

A business problem or an ambition is enough to start the conversation. We can help you explore possibilities, assess an existing idea or decide whether a simpler process improvement would be more useful.

Can a mid-sized organisation start with a limited scope?

Yes. We can focus on one team, one workflow or a shortlist of opportunities. We agree the scope, required input and deliverables with you before the work starts.

Who should be involved in an AI strategy engagement?

The mix depends on the question. It usually helps to involve the business owner, people who do the work, and colleagues responsible for data and technology. Leadership sets priorities and makes decisions about investment and ownership.

What happens if our data is not ready?

We identify which gaps affect the proposed use case and what needs to happen next. Through Algorhythm Group, we can bring in data engineering, architecture or analytics expertise when the agreed work requires it.
