The work

Connect the model to a decision.

We work with business and technical teams to define what needs to improve, how to measure it and how the result will be used.

01

Forecasting and planning

Use historical data to support demand, stock or capacity decisions. We consider the planning horizon, available inputs and the cost of different errors, then compare the model with the approach your team already uses.

02

Explainable machine learning and analytics

Explore the factors associated with an outcome and make the findings usable for business teams. We distinguish patterns in the available data from conclusions it cannot support, and explain uncertainty alongside the result.

03

MLOps and model operation

Help models move from analysis into repeatable deployment. Depending on the project, this can include data pipelines, model versioning, testing, monitoring and retraining workflows, with clear responsibilities for maintaining them.

Practical questions

Before we start.

How much historical data do we need?

There is no single minimum that applies to every problem. We assess the coverage, quality, frequency and relevance of your data against the decision and the patterns you need to understand. That assessment informs what can reasonably be built.

Do we need generative AI for forecasting or analytics?

Not necessarily. Statistical models, conventional machine learning or a clearer reporting process may suit the task. We choose the approach around the data and the decision, and compare it with an appropriate baseline.

Can you work with models our team has already developed?

Yes. We can assess existing models, their evaluation and their deployment requirements. Our MLOps case describes taking two models developed and patented by the client into production on an Azure foundation.

What if our data is spread across several systems?

We first assess which sources are needed and how they can be accessed and combined. When the work also requires data engineering, architecture or business intelligence, we can bring in complementary expertise through Algorhythm Group within an agreed scope.
